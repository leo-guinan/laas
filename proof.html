Outcome evidence, not better wording.

  • A baseline agreed with the business owner before the improvement begins.
  • A bounded factory or workflow with the work, burden, and correction surface named.
  • Independent readback showing what changed: time recovered, work removed, cost reduced, or opportunity unlocked.
  • Client confirmation or correction attached to the result.
  • Failures and unknowns preserved alongside the successful result.
