The problem

A good demo is not a reliable employee.

Your agent did something impressive once. Now you re-read everything it produces, because you can't tell the good outputs from the quietly wrong ones without doing the work yourself.

That isn't leverage. If you spend 40 minutes supervising an agent to save 45, you've built a very expensive intern.

The standard is in your head. Until it's written down and tested, your agent can't meet it and you can't trust it.

What we answer

Six questions about your agent.

  1. Can it do the job?Five real tasks, scored usable / needs correction / unusable.
  2. Can it do it well?We pull your taste out of your head with side-by-side comparisons: which is better, and why.
  3. Can it do it repeatedly?A written eval with 3–5 scored dimensions, run on every output, not just the good ones.
  4. Can you tell when it failed?We break it on purpose and check whether you, or it, noticed.
  5. Does it know when to ask?Explicit rules: act when X, ask when Y, stop when Z.
  6. Is it worth what it costs?Model spend plus your supervision time, against the work it actually removed.
