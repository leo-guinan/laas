# Canonical VSL script

Most people are using AI to do more work.

More content. More emails. More analysis. More automations. More things to maintain.

I think that’s backwards.

I’m a software engineer. I’ve spent about fifteen years building systems, and I was an early engineer at Copy.ai before ChatGPT took off.

And the thing I care about now is pretty simple:

**Can we use AI to make your business require less of you?**

Not theoretically.

Measurably.

I look at two numbers:

**time back and revenue in.**

If we remove five hours of recurring work, that counts.

If we create new revenue, that counts.

If I install some impressive AI workflow that gives you another thing to babysit, I don’t consider that leverage.

So the offer is simple.

You pay me $1,000.

I learn how your business actually operates, we agree on how we’re going to measure value, and then I start looking for work that can disappear without the result disappearing with it.

I work through the calibration with you until we’ve identified and implemented at least $1,000 of mutually agreed measurable leverage.

If it works and you want me to keep looking, it becomes $250 a month.

You don’t need to become an AI expert.

You don’t need to learn my system.

You work with me.

**The goal isn’t to help you work faster.**

**The goal is to make some of the work stop needing you.**

If that sounds useful, book the calibration below.
